Legibility, Sacrament and Glitch

Essays

I wrote this collection of short essays while I was focusing on academic backed research for "The Whale." The Whale is a triptych, so I’m structuring this collection of essays as a triptych as well. The first essay on legibility talks mostly about the physical and formal qualities of the work, the way it exists in space and why. The second essay on sacrament speaks on the conceptual and religious aspects of the work, as well as the qualities of the prose. Finally, the third essay on glitch talks about the video aspect of the installation.
