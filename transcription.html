The Whale is a work of translation. A lot of this work began to exist through small poetic lines in the margins of my class notes, collaged together into a big project. The poem was written first in prose, edited as I transcribed on the textile, and now for this transcription it has been translated into verse. The text was created for the textile format, so I've adapted it for online reading in a way I believe is loyal to the illegible qualities of the installation. The textile is not meant to be read linearly (in fact it's nearly impossible even if you try), so for this linear reading I have fictionalized some aspects, redacted aspects obscured in the original textile, and turned it into more of a narrative piece rather than fragmented like the original. This process of translation from prose to verse feels akin to the way I worked with the video for the installation, obsessively, iteratively, changing qualities for the presentation I prefer.
